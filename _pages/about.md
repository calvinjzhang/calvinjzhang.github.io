Hi, I’m Calvin Zhang! I’m a software engineer and researcher studying computer science and math at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/). I’m interested in the intersection of machine learning and computer systems, and I’m fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Giulia Guidi](https://giuliaguidi.github.io/) and [Prof. Johannes Paetzold](https://paetzold-lab.github.io/web/pi.html) in these areas.

My current work spans distributed and parallel programming for HPC and deep learning, as well as applications of reinforcement learning, AI agents, and deep learning in medical research. I also enjoy exploring the mathematical foundations underlying these fields, particularly optimization, probability, and linear algebra.
